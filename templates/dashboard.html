Obfuscation

URL obfuscation

Percent-hex escaped form
Homoglyphs (only works when copying as MARKDOWN and HTML)

Hostname percent-hex escaping encodes characters in the hostname using percent-hex notation.
Dot percent-hex escaping encodes the dots separating hostname labels as %2e.

Homoglyphs in scheme replaces protocol characters with visually identical Unicode lookalikes to bypass automated security filters while looking legitimate to users.

These techniques can help evade simple URL-based detection systems.

URL Spoofing

Redirect-Based (redirection notice)
Userinfo-Based

Redirect–Based wraps your URL in a known redirector to mask the final destination.
Userinfo-Based abuses the userinfo component of a URL to make a destination appear associated with a trusted domain.

When you click Copy URL, the endpoint URL is automatically obfuscated according to your selected setting.
